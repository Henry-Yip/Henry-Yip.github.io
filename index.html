Research

  • Summer 2026Hong Kong Observatory, summer intern. AI-based tropical cyclone track and intensity prediction: a machine-learning post-processing pipeline for global AI weather models that substantially reduces intensity forecast errors. First-author paper submitted.
  • Spring 2026Semester project, ETH Zürich. The resolution dependence of environmental tropical cyclone metrics, using ERA5 reanalysis.
  • Spring 2026Computational Statistical Physics final project, ETH Zürich. Critical exponents of the 3D Heisenberg model; it received full marks.
  • Summer 2025Summer research, School of Mathematics, University of Edinburgh. Synchronisation of small scales in two-dimensional turbulence with JAX-CFD, supervised by Dr Jacob Page, with assistance from Dr Dmitriy Zhigunov.
  • Summer 2024Summer research, School of Physics and Astronomy, University of Edinburgh. The three-body problem and symplectic integrators, supervised by Prof. Jenni Smillie.
  • 2023–24Research team leader, Edinburgh Scientific Researchers Association. Modelling liquid slosh with coupled pendulums and a wave equation.

Interests
