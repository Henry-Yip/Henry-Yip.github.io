Archive

Notes and short articles from 2022 to 2024, written while I was a student at the University of Edinburgh. Most are worked examples in physics and mathematics. In October 2026 I corrected the errors I found in them. Each post ends with a note on what was wrong and what changed; otherwise they are kept as they were.

Worth reading

A few I would still point people to:

  • Proving the figure-eight orbit exists: Chenciner and Montgomery’s action-minimisation argument on the shape sphere.
  • Sundman’s power series solution to the three-body problem: why a convergent series solution exists and why it is useless in practice.
  • Symplectic integrators: why they keep the energy error bounded over long runs.
  • Theorema Egregium: why every flat map is wrong.
  • Plane crash problem: how long does it take to fall?
