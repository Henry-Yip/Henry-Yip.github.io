PHYS08050 Introductory Astrophysics: Course Notes

1 July 2022, revised 25 October 2026. Level: undergraduate.

These are my notes for the University of Edinburgh course Introductory Astrophysics (PHYS08050), written with reference to the course book. Prof. Catherine Heymans kindly gave permission for my adapted notes to be shared here.

The notes assume calculus and Newtonian mechanics. They cover galaxies and cosmology, with some material on stars.

The notes have not been carefully proofread, so if you spot a mistake I would be grateful for a correction.
