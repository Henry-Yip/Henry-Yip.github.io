    PHYS08050 Introductory Astrophysics: Course Notes

    July 1, 2022 less than 1 minute read Undergraduate

    From the archive (2022). Corrected in October 2026; see the note at the end.

    On this page

    • Changes on 25 October 2026

    These are my notes for the University of Edinburgh course Introductory Astrophysics (PHYS08050), written with reference to the course book. Prof. Catherine Heymans kindly gave permission for my adapted notes to be shared here.

    Prerequisites: calculus and Newtonian mechanics.

    Scope: galaxies and cosmology, with some coverage of stars.

    The notes have not been carefully proofread, so if you spot a mistake I would be grateful for a correction.

    Download the notes (PDF)

    The \(\LaTeX\) source is reproduced on the sample files page as an example of a longer document.

    Changes on 25 October 2026

    What was wrong. Nothing on this page, although the introduction mostly apologised for errors in the notes.

    What changed. I rewrote the introduction, which now gives the prerequisites and the scope of the notes. The PDF itself is unchanged. Previously titled “PHYS08050 (Introductory Astrophysics) Course Notes”.
